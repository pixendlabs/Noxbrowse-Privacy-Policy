Noxbrowse Privacy Policy
========================

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Noxbrowse ("the Application", "We", "Us") is a Safari extension for iPhone, iPad and Mac that shows websites in a dark appearance. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Application handles and what it does not. In short: **websites are darkened on your device, we do not collect your browsing, and we do not require an account.**

Your browsing
-------------

-   Website colors are changed **on your device only**, inside Safari. Noxbrowse does not collect or send your browsing history, the addresses of pages you visit, page text, form entries, passwords or payment details.
-   The Safari extension only works on websites you allow in Safari's settings.
-   When you save a website rule, only its hostname (for example `example.com`, never the full address) is stored together with your appearance choices. Rules and settings stay on your device, in a storage area shared only between the Noxbrowse app and its Safari extension (an App Group).
-   A connection check stores only the time the extension last answered, so the app can show whether it is set up. It stores no address.
-   The sample article shown in the app is built into the Application and loads nothing from the internet.

Purchases
---------

**RevenueCat** manages the one-time "Noxbrowse Pro" purchase. It receives an anonymous app user identifier, your App Store purchase information and basic device information (such as the operating system version) to confirm that Pro is unlocked. Website content and website rules are never sent to RevenueCat. Noxbrowse does not receive your payment details; the purchase is processed by Apple.

Feedback
--------

If you choose to send feedback from inside the Application, the following is stored in **Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore)** so we can read it: your message, the category and the website hostname you choose to add, an anonymous install identifier, the app and system version, the device model, the language and whether Pro is unlocked. Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier for this and Firebase App Check confirms the request comes from the genuine Application. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

No analytics, no advertising
----------------------------

Noxbrowse contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDK. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy terms:

-   RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
-   Google (Firebase): https://policies.google.com/privacy
-   Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

What we do not collect
----------------------

We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any account. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Retention
---------

Feedback messages are kept as long as they are useful for improving the Application. Purchase records are retained by RevenueCat and Apple under their policies. Information stored on your device is removed when you delete the Application. To request deletion of data associated with your anonymous identifier, contact us at the address below.

Security
--------

Websites are processed locally. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data handled by third-party services.

Children's Privacy
------------------

Noxbrowse is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights
-----------

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the Turkish KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to its processing. As the Application keeps your settings on your device and uses anonymous identifiers, most of this can be done directly by deleting your website rules or the Application. For other requests, contact us.

Changes to this Privacy Policy
------------------------------

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above is revised.
